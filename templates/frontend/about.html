About Our Waste Management System

Our waste management system is designed to make reporting and tracking waste collection easy and efficient. We aim to create a cleaner environment by facilitating the proper disposal of waste.

Our Mission

To create a clean and healthy environment for our community through efficient waste management practices and community participation.

How We Work

User Reports

Users can report waste by providing details about the waste, its location, and an image. This information is sent to our waste management team.

Waste Collection

Our team reviews the reports and schedules waste collection. Once the waste is collected, the status is updated in the system.

Types of Waste We Handle

Organic Waste

Food waste, garden waste, and other biodegradable materials.

Inorganic Waste

Plastics, glass, metals, and other non-biodegradable materials.

Household Waste

General waste from households including mixed waste.

Mixed Waste

Combination of different types of waste that need sorting.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us. Our team is ready to help you!
